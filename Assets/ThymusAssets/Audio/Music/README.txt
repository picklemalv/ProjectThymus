Drop these sounds in this folder (see ../SOUND_LIST.txt):

  music_shelter.ogg   - Shelter: calm, simple, safe-feeling music.
  music_exploration.ogg   - Exploration: dark, slow tempo, lonely and unsafe.
  music_combat.ogg   - Combat: faster tempo, raises tension.
  music_high_contamination.ogg   - High contamination area: more intense, unstable music or ambience.
  music_final_boss.ogg   - Final boss: heavy, tense, big threat.
  music_ending.ogg   - Ending: after the final boss is defeated and the loop stops.
